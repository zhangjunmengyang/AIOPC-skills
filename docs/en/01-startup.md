# 🚀 Starting Up

Idea, validation, business model and positioning — decide what to build first.

---

## ⭐ Top picks

**[idea-refine](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/blob/HEAD/skills/idea-refine/SKILL.md)**

- *Why:* Turn a fuzzy idea into something testable.
- *First use:* Write three candidate ideas, each with audience, problem and outcome.

**[define-jtbd-canvas](https://github.com/product-on-purpose/pm-skills/blob/HEAD/skills/define-jtbd-canvas/SKILL.md)**

- *Why:* Start from the job the user is hiring you to do.
- *First use:* Fill one JTBD canvas with three real users.

**[business-model](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills/blob/HEAD/pm-product-strategy/skills/business-model/SKILL.md)**

- *Why:* See revenue, cost and channels on one page.
- *First use:* Fill all nine blocks and mark the two most uncertain.

**[pre-mortem](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills/blob/HEAD/pm-execution/skills/pre-mortem/SKILL.md)**

- *Why:* Find failure modes before you invest.
- *First use:* Assume failure in six months and list five causes.
